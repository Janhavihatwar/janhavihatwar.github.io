# Research & Projects

## Quantum Computing


### 1. Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) for Integer Factorization | IIT Bombay

Conducted master's thesis research on QAOA-based approaches to integer factorization. Implemented and benchmarked different QAOA variants using Qiskit, investigating their performance and exploring algorithmic modifications to improve factorization outcomes. This work led to the study of Variational Quantum Factorization (VQF) using different QAOA variations, accepted for presentation at COMSNETS 2025.
[Variational Quantum Factoring (VQF) using QAOA variations](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10885675/) — COMSNETS 2025, IEEE Xplore.

### 2. Quantum Error Mitigation for Variational Algorithms | QpiAI

Investigated and benchmarked quantum error mitigation techniques. Evaluated Measurement Error Mitigation (MEM), Zero-Noise Extrapolation (ZNE), and Pauli twirling on noisy multi-qubit GHZ circuits. Analyzed readout, depolarizing, dephasing, amplitude damping, and coherent gate errors using expectation values and state fidelity.

### 3. Benchmarking Quantum Algorithms on QpiAI Quantum Hardware | QpiAI

Designed and executed benchmarking experiments using quantum circuits and algorithms including GHZ states, W states, the Deutsch–Jozsa algorithm, and the Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE). Explored the execution and performance of these workloads on QpiAI Quantum hardware as part of evaluating practical quantum computing workflows.

### 4. Variational Quantum Eigensolver and QAOA for Molecular Ground States | QpiAI

Implemented and benchmarked VQE and QAOA for estimating the ground-state energy of the H₂ molecule. Explored different algorithm configurations and implemented a QAOA variant for the chain formulation of the problem.

### 5. Quantum-Assisted Protein Folding Using the HP Lattice Model | QpiAI

Implemented a quantum-computing approach to protein folding for the Gly–Ala–Gly peptide using the hydrophobic–polar (HP) lattice model. Explored the formulation of a simplified protein-folding problem for quantum optimization and the use of quantum algorithms to investigate candidate configurations.

## Experimental Physics

### 6. PICO Bubble Chambers: PICO-500 Detector Assembly and Data Analysis | University of Alberta

*Guided by Dr. Carsten Krauss, University of Alberta*

Worked on the next-generation PICO-500 detector assembly intended for eventual deployment at SNOLAB, Ontario, collaborating with a research team on detector preparation. Used MATLAB to analyze particulate levels within the detector, supporting contamination assessment and experimental quality control. Also gained experience with LabVIEW for developing an interface for detector communication.

### 7. Micropattern Gas Detectors | Guided by Dr. Archana Sharma, Senior Physicist, CERN

Studied the performance of micropattern gaseous detectors and radiation detectors through technical analysis and a review of scientific literature. Evaluated research papers to understand detector characteristics, experimental methodologies, and approaches to assessing detector performance.

## Data Science & Computational Projects

### 8. Network Analysis of the Indian Stock Market

Led a team of three in applying network science techniques to study relationships among 54 companies in the Indian stock market. Processed financial time-series data, calculated correlations between stock price movements, and visualized the resulting network to investigate interconnections and patterns in market behavior.

### 9. Comparative Study of Neural Networks for Financial Time-Series Prediction

Investigated neural network approaches to stock-price prediction by comparing convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and long short-term memory (LSTM) networks. Reproduced baseline results for a two-dimensional CNN, explored an alternative LSTM framework, and investigated feature selection and its effect on predictive performance.

### 10. Human Activity Recognition Using Smartphone Sensors

Explored human activity recognition using inertial sensor data collected from smartphones. Developed and evaluated machine-learning approaches for classifying activities, using data preprocessing, model validation, and visualization to investigate classification performance.

### 11. Hyperspectral Image Processing

Explored computational methods for hyperspectral image analysis through a review of existing image-processing techniques and the implementation of a research-paper methodology in Python. Investigated computational approaches for extracting and analyzing information from hyperspectral data.

### 12. COVID-19 Data Analysis and Trend Modeling

Developed a data analysis workflow using publicly available COVID-19 data from the Johns Hopkins Coronavirus Resource Center. Used web scraping, data processing, visualization, and machine-learning tools to investigate trends and model potential future outcomes.

### 13. Bitcoin Price Prediction

Investigated cryptocurrency price prediction using historical Bitcoin price data. Explored classification and regression models to analyze price patterns and evaluate predictive approaches for financial time-series data.
